# AllClaws Virtual Research Team

A simulated multi-platform collaboration experiment. Three virtual researchers, each running a different AI agent platform from a distinct architectural paradigm, collaborate on AllClaws research tasks.

## Team Members

| Member | Platform | Paradigm | Role |
|--------|----------|----------|------|
| **goclaw-operator** | GoClaw (Go) | Enterprise multi-tenant | Infrastructure & governance analyst |
| **clawteam-lead** | ClawTeam (Python) | Multi-agent swarm coordination | Architecture & coordination analyst |
| **hermes-researcher** | Hermes-Agent (Python) | Profile-based research agent | Research synthesis & cross-cutting analysis |

## Collaboration Model

Each member:
1. Picks tasks from the shared [TASKS.md](TASKS.md) board
2. Researches their assigned platform's approach to the task
3. Commits findings to their member directory as markdown
4. Coordinates via commit messages referencing other members' work

## Sprint Schedule

- **Sprint 1**: Baseline architecture analysis — each member documents how their platform handles workspace isolation, multi-task coordination, and security
- **Sprint 2** (planned): Cross-platform synthesis — members compare findings and produce unified analysis
