# Sprint 1 Task Board

## How This Works
Each virtual member claims tasks, does the research using the AllClaws architecture docs and platform source, and commits findings to their member directory. Tasks marked [SHARED] require all members to contribute.

---

## S1-T1: Workspace & Project Isolation Model [ASSIGNED: all]
**Status:** open
**Deliverable:** How does your assigned platform organize work into workspaces/projects/tenants? What is the isolation boundary? Compare to the other two paradigms.

## S1-T2: Multi-Task & Multi-Agent Coordination [ASSIGNED: all]
**Status:** open
**Deliverable:** How does your platform handle running multiple tasks concurrently? Inter-agent communication? Task dependency tracking? Parallel execution safety?

## S1-T3: Security & Credential Management [ASSIGNED: all]
**Status:** open
**Deliverable:** How does your platform handle credentials, sandboxing, audit logging, and access control? What is the trust model?

## S1-T4: Profile / Identity Model [ASSIGNED: all]
**Status:** open
**Deliverable:** Does your platform have a concept of user profiles or identities? How are different users/contexts separated? What persists across sessions?

## S1-T5: Cross-Platform Synthesis [ASSIGNED: hermes-researcher]
**Status:** blocked-by: [S1-T1, S1-T2, S1-T3, S1-T4]
**Deliverable:** After all members complete T1-T4, produce a synthesis comparing all three approaches. What are the trade-offs? What can each learn from the others?
